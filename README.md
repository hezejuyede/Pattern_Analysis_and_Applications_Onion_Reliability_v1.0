# Matched source replacement in plant image evaluation

The current research artifact is **v1.9.0**. Start with [v1.9/README.md](v1.9/README.md), the [current manuscripts](v1.9/manuscript/README.txt), the [additional descriptive analyses](v1.9/additional_analysis/README.md), and the [data/code licence notice](v1.9/DATA_LICENSES_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md).

This release retains corrected onion source-component controls, the second task with original author-recorded potato leaf identifiers, frozen ResNet18/DINOv2 and colour representations, and joint and single-source replacement experiments. It preserves all original model outcomes, prediction records, source assignments and numerical freezes.

The v1.9 revision adds comment-prompted descriptive analyses after the v1.8 results were known: complete leave-one-class-out and frequency-weighting sensitivity, class-specific correction/harm and available-error headroom, source support and recurring-target counts, and conditional allocation Monte Carlo standard errors. A separately implemented portable program scans all 4,704,230 saved prediction rows and rebuilds all fourteen new tables. No new classifier fitting, p-values or plant-population confidence intervals were added; the original all-class endpoint remains intact.

The contribution is a bounded empirical evaluation of local related-view response, sensitivity to the surrounding training policy and changes on never-exposed images. This release does not supply a newly collected field cohort, independently sampled whole plants, common pathological adjudication or verified absence of DINOv2 pretraining overlap. A research release is not an editorial decision or a declaration that authors have approved a new submission.

## Reproduction and reuse

The [v1.9 README](v1.9/README.md) gives exact integrity and reproduction commands. The original matched-replacement results and the fourteen additional descriptive tables have separate entry points. Per-file hashes, input bindings and lossless-conversion ledgers distinguish frozen original records from their distribution formats. The [v1.9 change record](v1.9/CHANGELOG_v1.9.md) explains the research and reproducibility changes.

The cached-input reconstruction is portable. [Source curation](v1.9/source_curation/README.md) separately documents upstream image requirements; [legacy training](v1.9/legacy_training/README.md) preserves historical regional/calibration scripts and inputs without claiming that full historical training was rerun for this revision. No private email, journal decision letters, original archive photographs or pretrained checkpoint files are distributed in v1.9.
