# v1.9 research and presentation changes

This record describes a revision after v1.8 results and a subsequent critique were known. It does not retrospectively preregister the additions or claim independent editorial approval.

- Preserved the v17/v18 model fits, raw prediction records, feature matrices, source assignments, pre-fit reviews and frozen numerical scripts. Unchanged copied objects are identified by SHA256 in `provenance/baseline_v1.8/COPY_LEDGER.csv`. The v1.8 scientific correction concerning filename-prefix grouping remains in effect.
- Added fourteen descriptive tables: source support, recurring-target frequencies and cross-split overlap; class-specific correction, harm and remaining-error headroom; repeated-context confusion counts; complete class-exclusion and frequency-weighting sensitivity; and conditional allocation Monte Carlo standard errors. The original all-class endpoint and original prediction space remain intact. There are no new fits, p-values or plant-population confidence intervals.
- Added a separately implemented portable reconstruction that reads all 4,704,230 stored prediction rows, rebuilds the new tables, and independently checks raw single-target outcomes against their saved summaries. Its actual run compared every table with the formal additional-analysis outputs; the maximum numerical difference was 1.4210854715202004e-14.
- Retained the original additional-analysis source unmodified, bound its fifteen historical inputs to portable objects, and separately bound the two raw single-source prediction files used for independent reconstruction. Gzip/Parquet transport keeps the original hashes and documented exact conversions.
- Updated release navigation and clarified the distinction between current descriptive additions, original experiments, external-context replay, historical regional/calibration training inputs and image-level source curation. Data-source terms remain unchanged; original project code is MIT, and upstream derived records retain their applicable notices.
- The current manuscript, supplement, figures, editable sources and presentation checks are maintained in their current release directories. Their document-specific changes and validation are recorded by the manuscript build. Historical numerical checks remain as provenance; they are not evidence that a journal has assessed this revision.

No new independent field cohort, whole-plant identities or pathological adjudication were obtained. DINOv2 pretraining/archive overlap remains unverifiable from the available evidence. Reproducibility checks do not remove these limits or guarantee external review or acceptance.
